<p style="text-align: justify; hyphens: auto;" lang="en">Populations, ecosystems and evolving lineages can shift abruptly from one state to another. I combine bifurcation theory, stochastic dynamics and time-series statistics to understand when such transitions can be anticipated from data, and when they cannot.</p>

<p style="text-align: justify; hyphens: auto;" lang="en">I am a PhD candidate in theoretical ecology, based jointly at the Institute of Ecology and Environmental Sciences of Paris (iEES-Paris, Sorbonne Université) and the Centre de Physique Théorique (CPHT, École Polytechnique), supervised by <a href="https://tomvandooren.eu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Tom Van Dooren</a> and <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/jeanrenechazottes/accueil" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Jean-René Chazottes</a>. I will defend my thesis, <em>Inferring resilience changes and critical transitions in population and evolutionary dynamics</em>, in November 2026. In March 2027, I will join <a href="https://www.epfl.ch/labs/bitbol-lab/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Anne-Florence Bitbol's Laboratory of Computational Biology and Theoretical Biophysics</a> at EPFL (Lausanne, Switzerland) as a postdoctoral researcher.</p>
